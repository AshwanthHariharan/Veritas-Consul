Who We Are

VERITAS CONSULTING is a professional advisory practice focused on Governance, Risk, Compliance, Assurance, Cybersecurity, Management Systems and Transformation.

Why VERITAS?

Built on the foundation of Truth, Integrity, and Trust

The Power of Truth

VERITAS is a Latin word meaning Truth — a timeless principle representing integrity, transparency, authenticity and trust.

Veritas Consulting was derived from this powerful idea: in a world of increasing complexity, organizations need clarity grounded in truth.

As organizations accelerate their adoption of Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, Agentic AI and cloud technologies, the traditional boundaries between technology, risk, security, compliance and business strategy are rapidly disappearing.

Veritas Consulting is positioned at this intersection — helping organizations innovate with confidence while governing with accountability.

Trust by Design
Security by Design
Privacy by Design
Governance by Design

That is where VERITAS CONSULTING stands.

We help organizations discover the truth about their risks, strengthen the trust in their systems, and build governance that enables innovation.

What We Do

Addressing complex challenges across the governance landscape

Our Expertise

VERITAS helps organisations understand and address the complex challenges arising from:

  • Governance and accountability requirements
  • Information and cybersecurity risks
  • Regulatory and contractual compliance
  • ISO management system implementation and transition
  • Internal controls and control effectiveness
  • Risk identification, assessment and treatment
  • Audit readiness and assurance
  • Business continuity and organisational resilience
  • Third-party and vendor risks
  • Privacy and data protection obligations
  • Emerging technology and AI governance
  • Management-system integration and continual improvement

Our role is not simply to identify gaps. We work with organisations to understand why those gaps exist, what risks they create, and how they can be addressed through practical, proportionate and sustainable solutions.
