ISO 42001: The New Standard for AI Management

January 2026 ISO Standards 6 min read

ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). It provides organisations with a structured approach to establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving how they govern and manage AI.

It is not simply an AI technology standard. It is a management-system standard for organisations using or providing AI systems.

Why Do Organisations Need an AI Management System?

AI introduces risks that traditional technology governance may not fully address.

An organisation may need to consider:

  • Bias and discrimination
  • Data quality
  • Privacy
  • Security
  • Transparency
  • Explainability
  • Accountability
  • AI system performance
  • Human oversight
  • Third-party AI services
  • Regulatory obligations
  • Unintended outcomes
  • Changes to AI models and systems

Managing these issues individually can quickly become fragmented.

An AI Management System provides a structured framework through which an organisation can bring these considerations together.

What Does Implementation Look Like?

Implementing ISO/IEC 42001 is not about producing a large collection of documents.

A successful implementation starts with understanding the organisation.

Step 1 — Understand the AI Landscape
What AI systems are currently being used? Who owns them? What processes depend on them? What data do they access? What decisions do they influence?

Step 2 — Establish Governance
Define responsibilities, accountability, policies and decision-making structures.

Step 3 — Identify AI Risks
Understand the risks associated with the organisation's AI systems and determine how those risks should be treated.

Step 4 — Establish Controls
Implement controls appropriate to the organisation's AI use cases and risk profile.

Step 5 — Monitor and Evaluate
AI systems should not simply be deployed and forgotten. Organisations need mechanisms to monitor performance, risks, incidents and changes.

Step 6 — Audit and Improve
Internal audits, management reviews and corrective actions help ensure that the system continues to evolve.
